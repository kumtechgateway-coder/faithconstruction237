OUR STORY

From a focused renovation team to a disciplined residential builder

FAITH Construction was founded with a simple ambition: raise the standard of residential construction in Cameroon through honesty, technical rigor, and execution that feels intentional from the first meeting to the final finish.

What began as a smaller operation centered on renovation work has developed into a premium construction practice trusted for homes and estates that feel durable, refined, and deeply personal to the people who live in them.

Design-aware execution

We protect architectural intent while making practical decisions that keep projects buildable and coherent.

Client-centered delivery

Built for longevity

We emphasize structural confidence, quality materials, and workmanship that continues to matter years after handover.

OUR JOURNEY

Key moments that shaped the company

Growth came through visible work, stronger teams, and a reputation earned over time rather than manufactured all at once.

2010

The beginning

FAITH Construction launched in Douala with a lean team and an early focus on residential renovation work rooted in reliability and word-of-mouth trust.

2012

First signature home

A flagship luxury villa project in Bastos marked a turning point, expanding visibility and setting a higher benchmark for the quality clients would come to expect.

2015

Regional expansion

Operations expanded to Yaounde and Bafoussam, and the company crossed the 100-project milestone with a broader residential portfolio.

2018

Industry recognition

Recognition for residential excellence reflected the company's growing reputation for premium work, detail, and dependable delivery.

TODAY

Still building with intention

With more than 200 completed projects and a team of 50+ skilled professionals, FAITH Construction continues to deliver homes and estates that balance ambition, structural confidence, and lived comfort.
