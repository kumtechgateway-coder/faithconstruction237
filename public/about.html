About FAITH CONSTRUCTION

Building Cameroon's premium residential future with integrity, excellence, and an unwavering commitment to quality since 2010.

OUR STORY

From Humble Beginnings
to Industry Leader

Founded in 2010 by Jean-Pierre Mbarga, FAITH Construction began as a small renovation company with a big dream: to transform Cameroon's residential construction landscape. What started with just three employees and a single truck has grown into the nation's most trusted premium construction firm.

Today, with over 200 completed projects and a team of 50+ skilled professionals, we continue to uphold the same values that built our reputation: integrity, craftsmanship, and client satisfaction. Every project we undertake is a testament to our commitment to excellence.

"We don't just build houses; we create homes where families will make memories for generations. That responsibility drives everything we do."

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional residential construction services that exceed client expectations, while maintaining the highest standards of quality, integrity, and professionalism. We strive to create spaces that enhance the lives of our clients and contribute to Cameroon's architectural landscape.

Our Vision

To be Cameroon's most trusted and sought-after premium construction company, recognized for architectural excellence, innovation, and unwavering commitment to quality. We envision a future where FAITH Construction defines the standard for luxury residential building in Central Africa.

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones That Defined Us

2010

The Beginning

FAITH Construction founded in Douala with just 3 employees. First project: a small home renovation in Bonapriso.

2012

Completed first luxury villa in Bastos, Yaoundé. Team expanded to 15 professionals.

2015

Nationwide Expansion

Opened offices in Yaoundé and Bafoussam. Reached 100 completed projects milestone.

2018
