FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services and process.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Simply fill out our contact form or give us a call. We'll schedule a consultation to discuss your project details, take measurements, and provide you with a detailed quote within 48 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We serve clients nationwide across Cameroon, with major offices in Douala, Yaoundé, and Bafoussam. Our team can travel to any location for consultations and project execution.

How long does a typical project take?

Project duration varies based on scope and complexity. A typical home renovation takes 3-6 months, while new construction can take 8-18 months. We provide detailed timelines during consultation.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes, we offer free initial consultations to discuss your project, understand your needs, and provide preliminary advice and estimates. Contact us to schedule yours today.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, mobile money (MTN/Orange), and structured payment plans tailored to your project timeline. Detailed payment schedules are provided in your contract.
